# Contributing

Automation Structures is open research. Claims state their evidence and assumptions, and I revise them when new evidence warrants a change. That approach guides contributions.

## Issues are the front door

The most valuable thing you can send is a well-supported challenge:

- a counterexample to a stated claim
- a system whose behavior the catalog cannot account for (a residual)
- an error in a paper, proof, model, or configuration
- a reproduction attempt that failed, with enough detail to diagnose

Questions about unclear definitions, confusing examples, and attempts to build something with the
structures are welcome too. Include the passage or example, what you understood it to mean, and
where you got stuck. You do not need a counterexample or a proposed repair to report a difficulty
with the explanation.

Open an issue for any of these. For a challenge to a research claim, the capstone paper states its
evidence and what would refute it. For a reading or construction question, a concrete account of
what you tried gives us a place to start.

## Pull requests

Small mechanical fixes, such as typos or broken links, are welcome as direct PRs.

Anything that materially changes the research is different. If you believe you have found a new structure, a reduction the minimality work missed, or a flaw in a retained claim, the contribution has to carry the same level of rigor as the rest of the project: a stated question, method, result, assumptions, and falsifier, with formal artifacts where the claim needs them. The promotion gate in [supplementary-research/README.md](supplementary-research/README.md) describes the standard that retained work meets.

I am selective about theory changes. The finer details are easy to misread, and a plausible-looking change that gets an obligation boundary or an ownership rule slightly wrong damages the whole structure. Open an issue and make the case before investing effort in a PR, and do not be surprised if the answer is that the work is interesting but does not enter the catalog.
